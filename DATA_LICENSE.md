# SHOHIN LUGHAT dictionary data licence

The dictionary is a combined dataset. Its components must be used under their applicable terms.

## Wiktionary-derived translations

Some entries are derived from English Wiktionary translation tables through the [Multilingualism project](https://github.com/MethodoxTech/Multilingualism).

Attribution: **Wiktionary contributors**, extracted via **MethodoxTech/Multilingualism** and Wiktextract / kaikki.org.

The upstream Multilingualism dataset declares its data under the **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)** licence. Those imported records retain this provenance in the `source` field. SHOHIN LUGHAT redistributes the combined dataset with attribution and under the applicable share-alike terms.

Licence text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

## Editorial entries

Entries authored for SHOHIN LUGHAT are identified by their own `source` metadata. They are not represented as native-speaker verified unless that review has actually been completed.

## Exclusions

Machine-translated rows from the upstream Multilingualism Tajik file were excluded from this import. No third-party proprietary dictionary has been copied into this dataset.
